COLD OPEN

INT. FINS & FEATHERS PET SHOP - DAY

A strip-mall pet store. Gurgling tanks, one annoyed parrot. Behind the register, MARGO KOWALSKI (50s, owner, has seen everything twice) does a crossword in pen. DEV (19, new hire, far too kind for retail) restocks hamster wheels.

The door chimes. TERRY BLOOM (40s, rumpled dad energy) rushes in cradling a goldfish bowl like a newborn. Inside, a goldfish floats belly-up.

                              TERRY
               Code orange. Code orange.

                              DEV
               Oh no. Is he okay?

Margo glances at the bowl. Back to her crossword.

                              MARGO
               He's upside down, Dev.

                              TERRY
               My daughter's back from her mom's
               at five. I need a Bubbles.

                              MARGO
                    (not looking up)
               Tank six.

Dev grabs a net and leads Terry to a tank of identical goldfish. Terry studies them like a police lineup.

                              TERRY
               He had a white spot. Left side.
               Like a little freckle.

Dev scoops one. Terry holds the bag next to the bowl. Squints.

                              TERRY (CONT'D)
               That's him. That's Bubbles.

                              DEV
               Sir, I'm so sorry for your loss.

                              TERRY
               Thanks. It's been a rough year for
               Bubbles.

Terry slaps a five on the counter and hurries out. The door chimes.

                              DEV
               That's so sweet. He'd do anything
               to keep his kid from getting sad.

                              MARGO
               His kid was in here Tuesday.

                              DEV
               Aw.

                              MARGO
               Bought a goldfish with a white spot
               on the left side. Said her dad gets
               really sad every time Bubbles dies.

Dev slowly turns to look at tank six.

                              DEV
               Wait. So how many times has Bubbles
               actually--

                              MARGO
               Bubbles died in February.

                              DEV
               We have to tell them!

Margo fills in a crossword answer. Calm.

                              MARGO
               That family is my entire margin.

The parrot squawks.
