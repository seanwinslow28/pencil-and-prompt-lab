                                                    COLD OPEN

    EXT. SUBURBAN CUL-DE-SAC - DAY

    Three police cruisers. A black tactical van parked across
    from a beige split-level with the blinds drawn.

    INT. TACTICAL VAN - CONTINUOUS

    Cramped. Monitors. Cold coffee. LT. MAUREEN PAZ (50s,
    reading glasses on a beaded chain) speaks into a headset.
    Her voice is warm milk. OFFICER TEDDY BLOOM (20s, rookie)
    watches her like she's performing surgery.

                        MAUREEN
              I hear you, Dennis. And I'm not going
              anywhere.

                        DENNIS (V.O.)
                  (filtered, sniffling)
              You said that an hour ago.

                        MAUREEN
              And look at that. Still here.

    A long beat. Static. Then:

                        DENNIS (V.O.)
              ...Okay. I'm coming out.

    On a monitor: DENNIS (40s, bathrobe, one slipper) shuffles
    out the front door, hands up. Officers close in. Teddy
    exhales like he's been underwater.

                        TEDDY
              How do you DO that? The voice?

                        MAUREEN
              Twenty-two years. Nobody can resist
              the voice.

    Her personal cell BUZZES. Caller ID: "LUCAS - DO NOT
    ANSWER." She answers.

                        MAUREEN (CONT'D)
                  (a shriek, no milk whatsoever)
              Where is my CAR, Lucas?

    Teddy flinches.

                        MAUREEN (CONT'D)
              No. You do not get to "technically"
              me. I MADE you.

    The van's rear door swings open. Two officers walk a
    cuffed Dennis past. He stops. Leans in, gently.

                        DENNIS
              Sorry, ma'am. He probably just needs
              to hear you're not going anywhere.

    Maureen stares at him. The officers lead him away.

    She closes her eyes. Breathes. Then, into the phone,
    pure warm milk:

                        MAUREEN
              Lucas. Sweetheart. I hear you.

    A beat.

                        LUCAS (V.O.)
              Are you doing the hostage voice on me
              right now?
