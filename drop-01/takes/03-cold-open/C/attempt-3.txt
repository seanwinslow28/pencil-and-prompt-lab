COLD OPEN

INT. WKRZ AM 1140 - BROADCAST BOOTH - MORNING

A cramped booth in a strip mall. Wood paneling. A "ON AIR" light that flickers. GLENN HOLLIS (60s, a voice like warm gravy) leans into a mic older than his producer.

TASHA OKAFOR (28, overqualified, holding a phone that says "LinkedIn") sits at the board.

                    GLENN
          ...and that's the Swap Shop. One
          lawnmower, lightly haunted. Now,
          Dunmore, you know what time it is.
          Caller number ten wins two tickets
          to the Muskingum County Fair!

He points dramatically at the phone board. Six lines. All dark.

                    TASHA
               (off mic)
          Glenn. We've never had ten callers.
          We've never had a caller number two.

                    GLENN
               (off mic)
          Four hundred loyal listeners, Tasha.
          They're shy.

A LIGHT BLINKS on line one. Glenn gasps. Grabs the phone.

                    GLENN (CONT'D)
          WKRZ, you're on the air!

                    PETE (V.O.)
          Hey, Glenn. Pete Vukovich, out at
          the water tower? Your antenna fell
          off.

                    GLENN
          ...Fell off.

                    PETE (V.O.)
          It's in my truck. Been meaning to
          call.

                    TASHA
          When did it fall off, Pete?

                    PETE (V.O.)
          March.

Silence. Tasha looks at the calendar on the wall. It says SEPTEMBER.

                    GLENN
          That's impossible. People hear us.
          Somebody hears us.

Tasha slowly leans toward the phone board. One tiny light, off to the side, has been blinking this whole time. LINE 2 - HOLD.

                    TASHA
          Line two's been on hold for...
               (squints at the display)
          ...a hundred and ninety-one days.

Glenn stares at it. Reverently, he picks up line two.

                    GLENN
               (softly)
          Hello? You're on the air.

                    DORIS (V.O.)
               (elderly, annoyed)
          I'm on hold, Glenn. Put me back.
          You're interrupting the show.

Glenn hangs up. Very carefully. Like it's a bomb.
