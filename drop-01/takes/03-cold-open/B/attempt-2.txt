INT. LOCKBOX ESCAPE ROOMS - LOBBY - DAY

A strip-mall storefront. Peeling vinyl on the window reads
"LOCKBOX ESCAPE ROOMS - CAN YOU GET OUT?" Behind a folding
table, DEV (34, lanyard, unearned confidence) watches a monitor
showing a family of four in a pirate-themed room.

PRIYA (36, his sister, coffee, already tired) enters and drops
her keys on the table.

                         PRIYA
          How long have the Hendersons been
          in there?

                         DEV
          Fifty-eight minutes. They're close.

On the monitor: the family sits on the floor. The DAD is lying
down.

                         PRIYA
          They're lying down, Dev.

                         DEV
          It's a strategy. They're thinking
          horizontally.

                         PRIYA
          Give them the hint.

Dev leans into a microphone.

                         DEV
          Ahoy, Hendersons. The Captain says
          look beneath the treasure.

On the monitor, the MOM lifts a plastic chest. Nothing under
it. She looks directly into the camera.

                         DEV (CONT'D)
               (off mic)
          Huh. That's where I put it.

                         PRIYA
          Put what?

                         DEV
          The key. For the door.

Dev pats his pockets. Slowly pulls a brass key off his lanyard.
Priya stares at him. He stares at the key.

                         PRIYA
          You have the key.

                         DEV
          I have A key.

                         PRIYA
          Is there another key?

                         DEV
          On the Captain's ring. In the room.
               (off her look)
          It's an escape room, Priya. I'm not
          going to keep the key OUTSIDE the
          room. That's not a challenge.

                         PRIYA
          So they can't get out and we can't
          get in.

                         DEV
          Which is honestly the best review
          we've ever had.

On the monitor, the Dad sits up and holds a napkin to the
camera. In marker: "WE HAVE A FLIGHT."

                         PRIYA
          Call the fire department.

                         DEV
          They said not to call them for this
          anymore.

                         PRIYA
          What did they say to call them for?

                         DEV
          Fires.
