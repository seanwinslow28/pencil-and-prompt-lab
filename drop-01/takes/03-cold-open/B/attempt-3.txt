COLD OPEN

INT. PARKING LOT - DRIVING SCHOOL SEDAN - DAY

A sedan with a "STUDENT DRIVER" roof sign crawls through an empty mall parking lot at four miles an hour.

Behind the wheel: TYLER, 16, hands locked at ten and two, knuckles white.

Passenger seat: DENISE, 52, driving instructor, sipping a gas station coffee with the calm of a woman who has died before.

DENISE
Good. Little more gas.

TYLER
How much is a little?

DENISE
Less than you're about to do.

Tyler lurches forward, slams the brake. The coffee sloshes. Denise doesn't blink.

DENISE (CONT'D)
See? You knew.

Her phone rings. She answers without looking.

DENISE (CONT'D)
(into phone)
Hi, Mom. Yes, I'm working. Yes, I'm in a car. That's the job.

TYLER
Should I pull over?

DENISE
Tyler, we're going four miles an hour. We are legally a bench.
(into phone)
Because he's not invited, Mom. Because he slept with his physical therapist. That's why Gary isn't coming to Thanksgiving.

Tyler slowly turns to stare at her. The car drifts toward a cart corral.

DENISE (CONT'D)
Eyes on the road. Not on my divorce.

Tyler snaps forward. Overcorrects. Brakes. The sign on the roof wobbles.

DENISE (CONT'D)
(into phone)
I have to go. I'm being murdered slowly.

She hangs up. A long, gentle silence.

TYLER
Sorry. About Gary.

DENISE
Thank you, Tyler.

TYLER
My mom says the best revenge is living well.

DENISE
Your mom's a smart woman.

TYLER
Wait. You know my mom?

DENISE
Tyler, I taught your mom to drive.

A beat. She looks at his hands on the wheel. Then back out the windshield.

DENISE (CONT'D)
So, no. Actually, she is not.

Tyler hits the gas. They lurch. She sips.
