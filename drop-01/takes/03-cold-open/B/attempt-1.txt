FADE IN:

EXT. BRENNAN DRIVING ACADEMY - STRIP MALL PARKING LOT - DAY

A dented sedan idles. The roof sign reads: STUDENT DRIVER - PLEASE BE PATIENT. Someone has added in marker: WE'RE TRYING.

INT. SEDAN - CONTINUOUS

KAYLA (16) has a death grip on the wheel. Passenger seat: MEG BRENNAN (34), clipboard, aggressively calm. Back seat: her father DALE (66), retired, uninvited, eating a sandwich.

                         MEG
          Deep breath. Before we move, what's
          the first rule of the road?

                         KAYLA
          Don't hit anything?

                         DALE
          Everybody else is an idiot.

                         MEG
          Dad. You're not the instructor anymore.

                         DALE
          I'm a customer.

                         MEG
          You're not paying.

                         DALE
          Then I'm family. Family rides free.
          Everybody else is an idiot, sweetheart.
          Write that down.

Kayla starts to write it down.

                         MEG
          Do not write that down. Mirrors.
          Signal. Ease out.

Kayla eases out. Then STOMPS the brake. Everyone lurches. Dale's sandwich hits the seatback.

                         KAYLA
          Squirrel.

                         MEG
          I didn't see a squirrel.

                         KAYLA
          It was looking at me.

                         DALE
               (re: the sandwich)
          That was turkey.

A HONK from behind. Dale cranks his window down and leans out.

                         DALE (CONT'D)
               (yelling)
          WHAT? SHE'S SIXTEEN! YOU WANT TO TRADE?

Meg hauls him back in by the collar.

                         KAYLA
               (small)
          Am I gonna pass?

                         MEG
          Kayla. Honey. I failed my road test
          three times, and now I own this company.

                         DALE
          Four.

                         MEG
          Three.

                         DALE
          Fourth time she hit the examiner.

                         MEG
          He walked into the car.

                         DALE
          He was walking TO the car, Meg. In
          the lot. The test hadn't started. She
          hit him during the part where you sit
          still.

Beat. Kayla slowly turns to Meg. Meg stares straight ahead.

                         MEG
          Ease out.
