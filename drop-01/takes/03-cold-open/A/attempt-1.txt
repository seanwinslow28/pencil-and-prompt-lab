                                                              THE COVENANT
                                                              "Pilot"

COLD OPEN

FADE IN:

INT. HONDA ODYSSEY - CUL-DE-SAC, HERON COURT - NIGHT

Parked across from a beige house. Inside the van: MARGARET FENN
(60s, fleece vest, thermos, the calm of a woman with a laminator)
and DENNIS PARK (40s, eating a sandwich over a napkin spread
across his lap like a surgical field).

Margaret lowers a pair of binoculars and writes on a clipboard.

                    MARGARET
          Nine forty-one. Subject's kitchen light
          is on.

                    DENNIS
          That's not a subject. That's Doug.

                    MARGARET
          Doug is the subject.

She hands him the binoculars. He looks, chewing.

                    DENNIS
          He's making a quesadilla.

                    MARGARET
          On a weeknight.

                    DENNIS
              (lowering them)
          Margaret, that's just a thing people do.

                    MARGARET
          Eleven days I've been on this structure.
          Eleven. No permit, no setback variance,
          no notification to adjacent homeowners.

                    DENNIS
          Or -- and I raise this gently -- we could
          ring the doorbell.

                    MARGARET
          And tip him off?

                    DENNIS
          He knows. You've been parked in his
          cul-de-sac for a week and a half in a
          minivan with a magnet on it that says
          H.O.A. COMPLIANCE.

                    MARGARET
          That magnet is deterrence.

                    DENNIS
          He waved at me Tuesday.

                    MARGARET
          Did you wave back?

                    DENNIS
              (long pause)
          I nodded.

Margaret shakes her head, disgusted. Dennis returns to his
sandwich.

                    MARGARET
          Does Sheila know where you are?

                    DENNIS
          She thinks I'm at a grief group.

                    MARGARET
          Why would you tell her that?

                    DENNIS
          Because she'd rather I was sad than here.

A porch light snaps on. Margaret sits up like a hunting dog.

                    MARGARET
          Movement.

Across the street, DOUG (50s, robe, Crocs) shuffles out to the
unpermitted structure and opens the door.

                    DENNIS
          What's he doing?

                    MARGARET
              (through binoculars)
          He's -- oh. Oh, Dennis.

                    DENNIS
          What.

                    MARGARET
          It's not a shed.

She is already out of the van, door hanging open. Dennis
scrambles to save his napkin.

                    DENNIS
          Margaret --

OFF SCREEN: a scuffle, a startled man, and the unmistakable
shriek of a chicken.

Dennis stares straight ahead, waiting. Chewing.

The passenger door opens. Margaret climbs in holding a live
hen. She buckles it into the middle seat.

                    DENNIS
          That's a chicken.

                    MARGARET
          That is a violation of Section Nine,
          Subsection D. Livestock, poultry, or
          exotic fowl.

                    DENNIS
          So you stole a chicken.

                    MARGARET
          I impounded it.

                    DENNIS
          You impounded a chicken.

                    MARGARET
              (starting the engine)
          It's in protective custody.

                    DENNIS
          From who?

                    MARGARET
          Its owner.

She pulls away at exactly fifteen miles per hour.
