Pencil & Prompt Lab The evidence room

Every take, playable and unedited.

Pencil & Prompt tests what AI can and can't take from real craft. This is where the experiments' raw material lives.

A Drop runs a new model on the same setup, on every model release. A Rig runs the same model on a new setup, between Drops. Each episode page shows every take exactly as the model returned it, with the verdicts and the method. The briefs stay sealed; each one is published as a SHA-256 fingerprint.
